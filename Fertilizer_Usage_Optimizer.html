About the Platform

What is the Fertilizer Usage Optimizer?

Fertilizer Usage Optimizer is a smart web application that helps farmers determine exactly which fertilizer to use, and how much, based on real soil test values and the crop being grown. Instead of guesswork or generic dosing, farmers get a tailored recommendation in seconds.

Why farmers need it

Over- or under-fertilizing is one of the biggest hidden costs in farming — this tool removes the guesswork.

Real cost advantages

Precise dosing means fewer wasted bags of fertilizer and a lower input bill every season.

Sustainable by design

Reducing excess fertilizer protects groundwater, soil microbiology, and long-term land value.

Built for real farms

From soil test values to a ready-to-use plan — no agronomy degree required.

    Everything you need to know before you get started.

    A recent soil test gives the most accurate results, but you can also use estimated values from your local agriculture office to get a directional recommendation.

    Wheat, Rice, Cotton, Maize, Soybean, and Sugarcane are supported today, with more crops planned based on farmer demand.

    We combine your soil deficiency levels with your field size in acres to calculate the number of kilograms needed for even, effective coverage.
